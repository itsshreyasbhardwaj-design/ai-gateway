# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be someone others want to work with. Assume good faith. Argue about the code,
not the person writing it.

## What that means in practice

**Expected:** thoughtful, specific feedback. Respect for the fact that people
have different experience levels, contexts and time. Graceful acceptance of a
"no". Focusing on what is best for the project rather than for being right.

**Not acceptable:** harassment of any kind, personal attacks, derogatory
comments, publishing others' private information, or sustained disruption.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space,
and to anyone representing the project publicly.

## Technical disagreement

Disagreeing strongly about a design is not a conduct problem — it is how good
software gets made. Making it personal is.

If a discussion stops being about the work, step away from it and bring it to
the maintainers.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers via
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/itsshreyasbhardwaj-design/ai-gateway/security/advisories/new)
or by opening an issue marked private if that is available to you.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will respond as promptly as they
can, may ask for detail, and will act proportionately — from a private word
through to a permanent ban for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this in good faith may face
consequences determined by the other maintainers.
